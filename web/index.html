You are watching the BLUE team. The match is a scripted replay — players follow a fixed script and do not react to the decisions. Every 2 seconds of match time there is a decision moment (a 2-minute match has 60). At each moment two different "tactics brains" look at the same situation and each picks one of 5 tactics for BLUE. We compare who picks the right tactic, how fast, and how many paid LLM calls each needs.

Brain 1 · our agent — Strands Decider 2B (small, fast model) picks the tactic; Amazon Nova Pro writes a coaching plan only when the tactic changes.
Brain 2 · LLM-only — a large language model (Amazon Nova) is asked at every moment, the usual way agents are built.
